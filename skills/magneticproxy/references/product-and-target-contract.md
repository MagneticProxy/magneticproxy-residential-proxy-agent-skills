# MagneticProxy product and target contract

Revalidate these facts against the active account and current official documentation before consequential use.

## Product role

MagneticProxy provides authenticated residential proxy transport, geographic routing, rotation, and sticky sessions. It does not supply a universal scraping, pricing, fraud-detection, or content-analysis API.

Choose the Capsule that matches the permitted workflow. General Purpose is suitable for small validation pilots; Web Scraping is intended for sustained structured collection; Price Monitoring is intended for repeated catalog observations. Do not purchase, change, or top up a plan without explicit authorization.

Do not promise access or suggest switching Capsules to evade the documented restriction; request a Product clarification for the specific Capsule and destination before writing a workflow around them.

Treat a documented restriction as terminal. Do not suggest alternate domains, endpoints, Capsules, or routing tricks to evade it.
